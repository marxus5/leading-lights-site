Where Your Support Goes

We’re not just investing in youth—we’re cultivating the next generation of changemakers. Every donation to our Memphis nonprofit becomes mentorship, training, and opportunity for a young person.

Mentorship and Leadership

Time with caring mentors and leadership training that help young people lead with purpose, resilience, and confidence.

Practical Life Skills

Real-world skills like money management, communication, and decision-making for confident, successful lives.

Social Communication Skills

Help for youth to build confidence, express themselves clearly, and connect through respectful communication.

Employability Skills

Problem-solving, teamwork, time management, and adaptability so youth can succeed in the workplace.

Digital Skills

The tools to thrive in a digital world, from online communication to coding and digital marketing.

Education and Entrepreneurship

Learning and critical-thinking skills, plus an entrepreneurial mindset that turns ideas into income.

Every Gift Lights the Way

Donate to Leading Lights today and help turn potential into progress. Today’s empowerment becomes tomorrow’s leadership.
