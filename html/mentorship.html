Youth mentorship that starts with connection

Leading Lights' youth mentorship in Memphis centers on caring relationships, consistent encouragement, and guidance shaped around each young person's strengths and goals. Mentorship can help youth build confidence, explore opportunities, and take practical steps toward a future they choose.

Listen and build trust

Make time for open conversation, listen without judgment, and create a dependable relationship where young people feel respected.

Explore goals together

Help youth identify interests, consider options, break goals into manageable steps, and notice the progress they make along the way.

Encourage and guide

Share perspective, model positive choices, celebrate effort, and connect youth with learning opportunities and other support when appropriate.

Effective mentoring is caring, consistent, and responsive to a young person's needs. Young people, families, schools, and youth-serving organizations can contact Leading Lights to ask about mentorship availability, referrals, or collaboration.
