Creative music learning for youth

Music Skills gives young people a space to explore creative expression, practice new techniques, and develop their own sound. Participants can build confidence while experimenting with music and sharing ideas in a supportive learning environment.

Writing Music

Develop musical ideas and learn how to shape them into original songs, melodies, and lyrics.

Mixing

Explore how to balance sounds, blend tracks, and create smooth transitions with audio mixing techniques.

Producing

Learn the creative process of building a track, arranging sounds, and developing a finished musical idea.

DJ

Practice selecting and blending music, keeping a flow, and creating an engaging listening experience.

Activities can be shaped around participants' interests and available resources. Youth, families, schools, and youth-serving organizations can contact Leading Lights to ask about program availability, referrals, or collaboration.
