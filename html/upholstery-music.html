Creative music learning for youth

Upholstery Music gives young people in Memphis a space to explore music skills through listening, rhythm, creative expression, practice, and collaboration. Youth can build confidence as they experiment with sound and share ideas in a supportive learning environment.

Listen and find the rhythm

Explore rhythm, melody, and musical patterns by listening closely and learning how different sounds work together.

Create and practice

Experiment with musical ideas, songwriting, voice, or instruments, then refine a piece through practice and feedback.

Share and collaborate

Learn to contribute to a group, take turns, respect different styles, and present creative work in a supportive setting.

Activities can be shaped around participants' interests and available resources. Youth, families, schools, and youth-serving organizations can contact Leading Lights to ask about program availability, referrals, or collaboration.
