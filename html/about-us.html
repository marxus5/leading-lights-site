AboutUs

Leading Lights is a Memphis, Tennessee nonprofit helping young people build brighter futures through mentorship, leadership development, practical life skills, and community support. We believe every young person deserves the guidance and opportunity to reach their potential.

SUPPORT How we help

Guiding Youth Toward Growth

We equip young people with caring mentorship, practical skills, and real-world opportunities so they can grow into confident, resilient leaders. By investing in youth today, we help build stronger families and a brighter future for our Memphis community.

Our Story

Leading Lights began with a simple belief: every young person deserves the guidance, tools, and opportunity to unlock their full potential. We bring that belief to life through supportive relationships, leadership development, and experiences that connect learning to future possibilities.

Our Vision

We envision a generation of empowered young people who lead with purpose, resilience, and confidence. Our work helps them discover their strengths, develop practical skills, and contribute to thriving communities.

Skills for everyday life and future goals

Alongside mentorship and hands-on programs, we help young people build a strong foundation for learning, work, and independent decision-making.

Practical Life Skills

Build confidence with useful everyday skills like money management, communication, and thoughtful decision-making.

Employability Skills

Practice problem-solving, teamwork, time management, and adaptability—skills that help young people prepare for the workplace.

Digital Skills

Develop digital confidence through online communication and exposure to areas such as coding and digital marketing.

Education Skills

Strengthen learning habits, critical thinking, and problem-solving to support academic and personal growth.

How We Support Our Community

Youth Freight Dispatching Class

Leading Lights recently completed a successful Dispatching class for youth, providing them with valuable skills in freight and logistics. This hands-on program offered real-world training, empowering young individuals with the knowledge and tools they need to pursue careers in the growing field of dispatching. By equipping them with industry-specific skills, we’re helping create a pathway for their future success in the workforce.

Community Feeding Program

Our Community Feeding Program is dedicated to serving those in need by providing nutritious meals to individuals and families facing food insecurity. Through this initiative, Leading Lights actively works to support and uplift the community, ensuring that no one goes hungry while fostering a spirit of compassion and solidarity. Together, we’re making a difference, one meal at a time.

Classroom Mentorship

Our Elementary Classroom Mentorship program is designed to inspire and guide young students, helping them build confidence, improve their academic skills, and develop a love for learning. Through hands-on mentorship, we provide support in the classroom, offering personalized encouragement and fostering positive relationships that empower students to succeed both academically and personally.

Celebrating Diversity Through the Arts

Our Cultural Performing Arts program celebrates diversity through the power of music, dance, and theater. By providing youth with the opportunity to explore and express their cultural heritage, we nurture creativity, self-expression, and a deeper appreciation for diverse traditions. This program not only enriches their artistic skills but also fosters unity, pride, and respect for all cultures within the community.

Re-entry

Our Re-Entry Support Program is dedicated to helping individuals successfully transition back into society after incarceration. We provide mentorship, job training, educational resources, and community support to ensure a smooth reintegration. By empowering participants with the tools and skills needed to rebuild their lives, we help foster a positive path forward and reduce recidivism.

Apprenticeship & Life Skills Program

Our Apprenticeship Program offers hands-on training and mentorship in various trades and industries, providing youth with the skills and experience needed to jumpstart their careers. Through real-world learning, we connect participants with industry professionals, giving them valuable insights and a clear path to employment. This program helps bridge the gap between education and career, empowering the next generation of skilled workers.

Help Us Keep Showing Up

Your support keeps these Memphis youth programs and community outreach efforts going, putting mentorship, practical skills, and opportunity in the hands of more young people.
