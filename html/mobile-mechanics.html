Hands-on automotive skills for youth

Mobile Mechanics introduces young people in Memphis to automotive basics through guided, safety-conscious learning. Youth can explore how vehicles work, practice problem-solving, and discover technical education and career pathways without assuming prior experience.

Tools and vehicle systems

Get familiar with common automotive tools and the basic purpose of major vehicle systems. Learn how to ask questions, follow instructions, and identify when a task needs an experienced technician.

Maintenance and troubleshooting

Practice observation and step-by-step thinking through introductory vehicle checks and maintenance concepts. Learn how technicians use information and testing to narrow down a concern.

Safety and career awareness

Put safe work habits first, recognize the limits of beginner practice, and discover how technical education, supervised experience, and industry credentials can connect to automotive careers.

Hands-on vehicle work should take place with appropriate supervision, equipment, and safety procedures. Youth, families, schools, and youth-serving organizations can contact Leading Lights to ask about program availability, referrals, or collaboration.
