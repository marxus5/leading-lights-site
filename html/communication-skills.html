Communication and life skills for young people

This youth communication skills program in Memphis focuses on everyday tools for school, work, and relationships. Young people can practice listening, expressing ideas, understanding perspectives, and working through challenges with others.

Listen and understand

Practice active listening, asking thoughtful questions, noticing nonverbal signals, and checking that a message has been understood.

Speak with confidence

Organize thoughts, share opinions respectfully, make requests, and communicate with different people and in different settings.

Work through challenges

Build habits for collaboration, perspective-taking, constructive feedback, and handling disagreement without losing respect for one another.

Activities can include guided discussion, role-play, group projects, and reflection. Youth, families, schools, and youth-serving organizations can contact Leading Lights to ask about program availability, referrals, or collaboration.
